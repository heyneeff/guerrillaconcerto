Sponsorship Dossier

Candidate brands for the wildlife-on-horseback film project, sorted by category and how realistic a first "yes" is. Built from the Wyoming wild horse expedition footage as proof of concept.

base: Boulder / Denver, CO proof of concept: Wyoming wild horse expedition updated: Aug 2026

Regional advantage

An unusual number of outdoor and equestrian brands are headquartered within a few hours of Boulder/Denver — Steamboat Springs, Fort Collins, Lafayette, Cortez, and Jackson Hole all show up below. Brands marked local are close enough for an in-person visit instead of a cold email, which is almost always the faster route to a first deal.
